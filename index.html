WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing. MicroNet is a static site. There is no server, no database, no analytics, and no tracker. Your searches are computed entirely in your browser using the local sites.json file.

PIXLECT ACCOUNT

If you sign in with PIXLECT, your session is encrypted and stored in your browser's localStorage under the key microneet:pixlect_user. It never leaves your device unencrypted.
